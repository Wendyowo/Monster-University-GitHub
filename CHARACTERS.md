# Monsters University Characters

A guide to the main characters from Disney/Pixar's *Monsters University* (2013).

## Main Characters

### Mike Wazowski
- **Voiced by:** Billy Crystal
- **Major:** Scaring
- **Description:** A small, round, green monster with one big eye. He has wanted to be a Scarer since childhood and makes up for his lack of natural scariness with hard study and determination.

### James P. "Sulley" Sullivan
- **Voiced by:** John Goodman
- **Major:** Scaring
- **Description:** A large blue-and-purple furry monster from a famous scaring family. He starts out relying on talent and his family name, then learns to work hard and respect others.

### Randall "Randy" Boggs
- **Voiced by:** Steve Buscemi
- **Major:** Scaring
- **Description:** Mike's first roommate, a lizard-like monster who can turn invisible. He begins as a friendly, nervous student and later joins Roar Omega Roar, setting up his rivalry with Sulley.

## Oozma Kappa (OK) Fraternity

### Scott "Squishy" Squibbles
- **Voiced by:** Peter Sohn
- **Description:** A shy, many-eyed monster who still lives at home with his mom. The OK house is his mother's home.

### Don Carlton
- **Voiced by:** Joel Murray
- **Description:** A middle-aged former salesman who went back to school. He has suction-cup tentacles and is the fraternity's president.

### Terri and Terry Perry
- **Voiced by:** Sean Hayes (Terri) and Dave Foley (Terry)
- **Description:** Two heads that share one body. Terry is older, sarcastic, and in charge; Terri is more upbeat and likes to dance.

### Art
- **Voiced by:** Charlie Day
- **Major:** New Age Philosophy
- **Description:** A purple, arch-shaped monster who is odd, free-spirited, and easy to get excited.

### Sonia Squibbles
- **Voiced by:** Julia Sweeney
- **Description:** Squishy's cheerful mother, who supports the fraternity and lets the members live in her house.

## Roar Omega Roar (ROR) Fraternity

### Johnny Worthington III
- **Voiced by:** Nathan Fillion
- **Description:** The confident, arrogant president of ROR, the top fraternity on campus, and the main rival of Oozma Kappa.

### Chip Goff
- **Voiced by:** Bobby Moynihan
- **Description:** A big ROR member who is often seen alongside Johnny.

### Javier Rios
- **Description:** A ROR member with several eyes and tentacle hair.

### Reggie Jacobs
- **Description:** A ROR member who often appears with the group.

### Chet Alexander
- **Description:** A ROR member and one of Johnny's followers.

## Faculty and Staff

### Dean Abigail Hardscrabble
- **Voiced by:** Helen Mirren
- **Description:** The strict dean of the School of Scaring. She has a centipede-like body and bat-like wings, and is a scaring legend who holds the all-time scare record.

### Professor Derek Knight
- **Voiced by:** Alfred Molina
- **Description:** The Scaring 101 professor who teaches the scaring program's core class.

### Claire Wheeler
- **Voiced by:** Aubrey Plaza
- **Description:** President of the Greek Council, who co-hosts the Scare Games.

### Brock Pearson
- **Description:** Greek Council vice president, who co-hosts the Scare Games with Claire.

## Other Characters

### Carrie Williams
- **Description:** President of the sorority Python Nu Kappa (PNK).

### Frank McCay
- **Voiced by:** John Krasinski
- **Description:** A Monsters, Inc. Scarer who inspires a young Mike to become a Scarer during a school field trip.

### Archie the Scare Pig
- **Description:** Fear Tech's mascot. Sulley steals it, and Mike chases it across campus.
